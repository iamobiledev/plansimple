# ADR-010: Prefer Bluebeam Revu behavior on ambiguity

## Status
Accepted

## Context
Product brief is large; agents may restart without memory.

## Decision
When a UX/tool behavior is ambiguous, choose the option closest to Bluebeam Revu and record it in `docs/PROGRESS.md`.

## Consequences
- Faster decisions, less stalling
- Occasional intentional divergence must be documented
